Ludo Carnival Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Ludo Carnival game for Android ("the app") uses, and why. The app is made by Jedosoft Apps ("we", "us"). If you have any question, email us at jedosoftnp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up or account. We do not run any servers and we do not collect your personal information.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

To play, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • the player name you type in (it can be any nickname)
  • coins, items you unlock, your level and game statistics
  • your settings (sound, theme, house rules and similar)
  • an unfinished match, so you can continue it later

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with Me → Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. This includes banner ads and optional "watch a video" reward ads, which you choose to start. To deliver ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • device and app information (for example phone model, operating system version and language)
  • how you interact with the ads (for example ad views and taps)

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, and (only where you allow it) personalise ads. This information goes to Google, not to us. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice any time in Me → Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Reward ads are optional. You never have to watch a video to play.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads. It does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Your game data stays on your device. Ad requests to Google are sent over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
